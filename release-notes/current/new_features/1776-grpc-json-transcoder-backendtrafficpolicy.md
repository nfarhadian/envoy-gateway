Added a `grpcJsonTranscoder` field under `BackendTrafficPolicy` that enables Envoy's gRPC-JSON transcoder, letting REST/JSON clients call gRPC backends. The proto descriptor is referenced from a ConfigMap and is validated at translation time, so a malformed or incomplete descriptor surfaces as a policy status condition instead of a rejected listener. Descriptors must be generated with `protoc --include_imports`. When `services` is omitted, every service declared by the descriptor's own proto files is transcoded, excluding services from imported files.
